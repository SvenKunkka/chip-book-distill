    Chapter 04

    A desk, a bookshelf, and words carved in stone

    Three kinds of memory. Cut the power and the difference is obvious.

    Power ON

    Working memory

    Like a desk. What you are using, within reach.

    • Fastest storage on the chip
    • Empty again the moment power stops
    • Costs the most area per kilobyte
    Static random-access memorySRAM

    Program memory

    Like a bookshelf. Put away, kept, taken down again.

    • Keeps its contents with no power
    • Holds the software and the settings
    • Needs extra manufacturing steps
    Flash memory, a non-volatile memoryNVM

    Permanent instructions

    Like words carved in stone. Written once.

    • Written at the factory, never again
    • Holds the code the chip boots from
    • The smallest storage per bit
    Read-only memoryROM
    Behaviour is what matters for a product decision, not device physics.
